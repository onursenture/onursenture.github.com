/resume.pdf: what does the paper look like?

A4, one page as the goal (two at most), real selectable text with the fonts embedded. The three options differ in how much of the site's identity carries over to paper. The draft copy is the same in all three. Click one.

A

The site on paper (my pick)

The same label | content columns as the web page, in Plex Mono with the name in Plex Sans. Hairline rules, black ink, and the accent only on links. Anyone who has seen the site recognises it, and it stays a quiet, single-column text flow, so ATS parsers read it fine.

B

Plain, ATS-first

All Plex Sans, ruled section heads, no label column, no colour. Recruiters' systems parse it the most reliably and it's the most familiar shape, but it says nothing about you as a designer.

C

Branded

A dither strip at the top, the name in Doto, accent labels. It's the most "you" on paper, but in a stack of PDFs it's also the loudest, the dither has to be embedded as an image, and grayscale printing turns it to mud.
