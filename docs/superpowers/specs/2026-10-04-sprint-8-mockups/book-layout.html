/book/: how do you pick a call type?

Three call types on the site grid. The cal.com calendar (light theme, ultramarine brand colour, cal.com's own event details hidden) opens on the same page. The options differ only in how you choose a type and where the calendar shows up. The copy and durations are drafts. Click one.

A

Rows that open in place (my pick)

Three hairline rows. Choosing one opens the calendar under it, and the others stay visible as rows, so you can switch with one click. The choice lives in the URL hash (/book/#project), so it can be shared, the page stays one static HTML, and the CDN cache holds. Home and resume links can point straight at a type.
