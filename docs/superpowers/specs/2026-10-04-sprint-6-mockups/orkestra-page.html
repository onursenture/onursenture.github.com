An Orkestra page, and the look of the "Then" block

Nebuu as the example, with real facts from the research and draft copy. The page is the same product page as PrimeTek (header, then blocks on the site grid), plus two Orkestra additions: Live links in the facts, and the new Then block straight after the header. The three options differ only in how the Then block looks. Click one.

A

Year stamp in the label column (my pick)

The label column holds "Then" and, under it, the year set large in Doto, the dot-matrix face we already use for display numbers. The text sits in the 480px column like any text block. It's the quietest option, and the big year carries the "this was 2013" feeling on its own.
